# Claims Reserving Valuation — Governance Review Log
Reserving committee archive for the interrupted year-end loss-development valuation (2026-Q1 through 2026-Q2).

## Executive Summary
How the reserving engine is *meant* to behave — the rebuild of the cumulative development triangle from the incremental movement extract, ratio eligibility, factor selection, the tail, the chaining of development factors, the projection base, the expected-loss method, the credibility blend, reserve and IBNR, queue admission, tiering, ordering and the reviewer capacity cap — was settled incrementally by the reserving committee, and those decisions live in the review notes below rather than in any single summary. Several conventions deliberately depart from the textbook chain ladder: the selection is neither all-year nor volume weighted, the tail is derived rather than assumed, the rounding direction is fixed separately for each stage, and the reserve and the IBNR are floored differently. The winter draft proposals were revisited during the 2026-05 committee review and several were reversed; where a draft or an interim conflicts with a later decision, the later dated decision governs. `/app/docs/report_spec.json` is the output contract only.

> **Reserving committee decision (2026-05-04 - #RSV-4104)** Adeyemi: factor selection, final (this supersedes the volume-weighted draft #RSV-4004 and revises the plain-average interim #RSV-4102). Form the individual age-to-age ratios for a development transition as exact rationals, one per accident period. When FIVE or more individual ratios survive eligibility at that transition, first remove the single highest and the single lowest of them — where two ratios tie for highest, or for lowest, the earlier accident period is the one removed. From what remains keep only the LATEST THREE by accident period. The selected factor is the simple arithmetic mean of those kept ratios expressed in basis points. A transition where no ratio survives selects 10000 basis points, i.e. no development. ROUNDING: selected_factor_bp = HALF-UP, ties resolving upward

The cell at development lag L holds the CUMULATIVE sum of every increment at lags 0 through L, so a lag with no movement at all repeats the previous cumulative figures unchanged and is never written as zero. Every line named by the calendar carries every accident period up to the valuation period, each with development lags 0 through the valuation diagonal; line keys and accident-period keys are sorted ascending and cells are ordered by dev_lag ascending.
